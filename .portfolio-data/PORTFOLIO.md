# SirBepy Blog

## The What

A personal blog covering AI-assisted development, vibecoding workflows, and lessons from building projects from scratch. Written with beginners in mind - people just starting to explore how AI can be a real development partner rather than just an autocomplete.

## The Why

Most AI/coding content online is either too surface-level ("here's how to use ChatGPT") or too advanced for someone just dipping their toes in. This blog fills the middle ground: honest, practical write-ups from someone actively experimenting, aimed at helping a beginner get their bearings faster.

## The How

Plain HTML with no build step - intentional. The blog itself is an argument that you don't need a framework to ship something real. Each post is a standalone HTML file, making it easy to author, version, and deploy without tooling overhead.
